Sub Attack! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sub Attack! (the “Game”) handles information when you use the Android app. If you have questions, contact app.support.pjconsulting@gmail.com.

1. Information processed when you play

The Game may store gameplay progress, high scores, achievements-related progress, settings, and Premium status on your device to support gameplay and restore your preferences. Depending on the services enabled and used, third-party providers may process information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and purchase or account-related information as described below.

2. Google Play Games Services

If you choose to use Google Play Games features, Google may process your Play Games profile information, player identifier, achievements, leaderboard scores, and related activity. These features are operated by Google under its own privacy terms. The Game may use the information made available by Play Games to display and update your game achievements and scores.

3. Advertising and rewarded ads

The free version of the Game may display advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements that provide an in-game benefit. When advertising is enabled, Google's advertising services (such as Google AdMob) may process information including advertising identifiers, device and app information, approximate location inferred from technical signals, ad interactions, and diagnostics for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and protecting ads. Advertising data practices and any consent choices depend on your location, device settings, and the advertising services in use.

See Google's Privacy Policy for more information. You may be able to manage advertising preferences through your Android and Google account settings.

4. Premium purchases

The Game offers an optional one-time Premium upgrade through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and transaction details. The Game may receive information needed to determine whether the Premium entitlement is active, such as purchase status or a transaction token. The Game does not need to receive your full payment-card details to unlock Premium features.

5. How information is used

Information processed by the Game or integrated services is used, as applicable, to operate gameplay, save preferences and progress, provide achievements and leaderboards, display and measure advertising, validate Premium purchases, prevent fraud, and maintain performance and security.

6. Sharing and service providers

The Game uses or may use Google services including Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, and Google advertising services. Those providers process data according to their applicable terms and privacy policies. The Game does not offer a feature for players to publicly post personal information to other players; leaderboard information may be visible through Google Play Games according to your Google settings.

7. Storage and retention

Game progress and preferences saved locally generally remain on your device until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall it, subject to any device backup or restore functionality. Information held by Google or other integrated service providers is retained according to their own policies. Clearing local app data does not necessarily remove information stored in your Google account or provider systems.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop playing the Game and uninstall it at any time. You may clear locally stored app data through Android settings. Google account, Play Games, advertising, and purchase-related privacy controls are managed through the relevant Google services. For questions or requests relating to information handled directly by the Game, email app.support.pjconsulting@gmail.com. We will review requests and respond as applicable.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact the email above. The Game's intended audience and any age-related requirements are governed by its Google Play listing and applicable platform settings.

10. Security

We aim to limit information handled by the Game to what is necessary for its features. No system can guarantee absolute security. Third-party services apply their own security measures to information they process.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this webpage, with the date above updated when changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, please email app.support.pjconsulting@gmail.com.

Note: Google Play's Data safety declaration must accurately reflect the data practices of the specific SDKs and app version you publish. This policy should be reviewed and updated if advertising, analytics, sign-in, or other data-handling features change.
